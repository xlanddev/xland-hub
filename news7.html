Xland Video Center Has Arrived

Xland Support introduces a new video platform for long-form videos, Shorts and developer content.

📅 August 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read 👤 Xland Team

🎬 A New Video Experience for Xland

Xland Support is introducing a new Video Center designed to make Xland videos easier to discover, watch and interact with.

The new system supports both long-form videos and Shorts, creating a dedicated space for tutorials, project updates, news, development content and other Xland videos.

"Xland videos now have their own home."

🚀 What Has Been Added?

The new Xland Video system introduces several new features to Xland Support.

🎬 New Video Features

  • 🎥 Long-form video support
  • 📱 Shorts video support
  • ▶️ Dedicated video player
  • 👁️ Video view counter
  • 👍 Video likes
  • 💬 Video comments
  • 🔗 Video sharing
  • 🖼️ Video thumbnails
  • 📂 Automatic video loading from JSON
  • ⚡ Dedicated video pages

📂 Videos Are Managed Through JSON

One of the most important parts of the new system is the way videos are managed.

Instead of manually editing the HTML page every time a new video is published, Xland Support now uses a dedicated videos.json file.

When a new video is added to the JSON file, the video.js system automatically reads the information and creates the video card on the website.

"Add the video to JSON. Xland Support handles the rest."

🎥 Long Videos

The top section of the Video Center is dedicated to long-form content.

This section can contain tutorials, development videos, project demonstrations, announcements and other longer videos.

🎬 Long Video System

  • 🖼️ Custom thumbnail
  • ▶️ Play button
  • 📝 Video title
  • 👁️ View count
  • 👍 Like count
  • 📄 Dedicated video page

📱 Xland Shorts

Below the long-form videos, Xland Support now includes a dedicated Shorts section.

Short videos use a vertical 9:16 format and are designed for quick tutorials, announcements, clips and Xland moments.

📱 Shorts Features

  • 📱 Vertical 9:16 layout
  • ▶️ Dedicated Shorts player
  • 👍 Like button
  • 💬 Comments
  • 👁️ Views
  • 🔗 Share functionality

▶️ Dedicated Video Player

Every video can now open its own dedicated player page.

Users can click the video thumbnail or Play button to open the video page, where the video can be played normally using the browser's video player.

"Click the video. Open the player. Start watching."

💬 More Interaction

The Video Center is not only about watching videos. Users can also interact with videos through likes, comments, views and sharing.

📊 Video Interaction

  • 👍 Like videos
  • 👁️ Track video views
  • 💬 Write comments
  • 🔗 Share videos

⚙️ Built for Future Expansion

The Video Center was designed so that adding new videos does not require rebuilding the entire page.

The JSON-based architecture makes it possible to continue adding videos while keeping the website structure clean and organized.

Future versions can also expand the system with additional video categories, improved discovery tools and more community features.

🚀 What's Next?

The Xland Video Center is only the beginning.

Future updates may introduce more video content, additional Shorts, improved interaction features and deeper integration with the Xland ecosystem.

🔮 Future Possibilities

  • 🎬 More Xland videos
  • 📱 More Shorts
  • 🔎 Better video discovery
  • 📊 Advanced video statistics
  • 👤 Creator features
  • 🔔 Video notifications
