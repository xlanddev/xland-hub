Xland Hub 5.2 Has Arrived

Xland Hub 5.2 is here — bringing Xland AI Copilot, a smarter ecosystem navigation experience, major mobile improvements, a redesigned Xland Script experience and another major step toward a more connected Xland ecosystem.

📅 September 2026 ⏱️ 15 min read 👤 Xland Team

🚀 Welcome to Xland Hub 5.2

Xland Hub 5.2 has officially arrived.

Following the expansion introduced in Xland Hub 5.1, version 5.2 focuses on improving how users interact with the Xland ecosystem.

This release introduces a new Xland AI Copilot directly inside the Home page, major mobile improvements, a redesigned Xland Script experience and several navigation and interface improvements across Xland Hub.

"Xland Hub 5.2 — Smarter. Faster. More Connected."

🔥 What's New in Xland Hub 5.2

Xland Hub 5.2 is one of the larger interface-focused updates to the platform.

Instead of simply adding another page, this release focuses on making the existing Xland ecosystem easier to discover, navigate and use across different devices.

🤖 Xland AI Copilot

One of the biggest additions in Xland Hub 5.2 is the new Xland AI Copilot.

The Copilot is integrated directly into the Home page and provides a dedicated interface for discovering different parts of the Xland ecosystem.

Users can search for Xland projects, developer tools, games, news, support pages, maps and other ecosystem experiences without manually searching through the navigation menu.
